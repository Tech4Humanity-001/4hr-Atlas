# Atlas Course Engine — Requirements

## RQ-001 Source preservation
Preserve all authoritative Atlas records, canonical IDs, existing correct content and provenance.

## RQ-002 Corpus integrity
Validate the complete canonical corpus and prove the expected 489 subtopics and 516 research-story records or document any verified source discrepancy.

## RQ-003 Learning product
Represent the full one-hour lesson lifecycle: introduction, context/discussion, example, lesson, practice/activity, assessment, marking, feedback, mastery, credential and post-activity/action.

## RQ-004 Choice and adaptation
Represent standard pathways, choice/dip paths and personalised/adaptive pathways without making adaptive behaviour mandatory where it is not implemented.

## RQ-005 Content
Support reusable text, scripts, transcripts and structured learning content with provenance and versioning.

## RQ-006 Media/assets
Support text, audio/voice, image/diagram/illustration, video/animation, storyboard, captions, worksheet, presentation, interactive/simulation, assessment, social content, email and credential assets.

## RQ-007 Activities
Support learning activities independently of assessment: practice, reflection, application, scenarios and actions.

## RQ-008 Assessment
Support diagnostic, formative, quiz, adaptive, summative, practical, scenario, reflective, project, portfolio, peer and AI/human-assisted marking patterns as extensible capability classes.

## RQ-009 Marking/feedback
Record marking rules, outcomes, feedback and evidence; never infer mastery from the existence of a question bank alone.

## RQ-010 Mastery
Represent mastery rules independently from a particular exam implementation.

## RQ-011 Credentials
Support achievement rule → verification → credential → record → recognition. Credential evidence may be completion, mastery, project, portfolio or pathway achievement.

## RQ-012 Delivery
Support catalogue, enrolment, authentication, learner portal/player, lesson delivery, notifications, accessibility and support through the chosen delivery layer.

## RQ-013 Learner lifecycle
DISCOVER → ENROL → ORIENT → LEARN → PRACTICE → ASSESS → REFLECT → MASTER → CREDENTIAL → APPLY → FOLLOW-UP → RETURN.

## RQ-014 Measurement
Represent involvement, satisfaction, novelty, completion, persistence, time-on-task, pathway choice, assessment performance, mastery, confidence, application, behaviour/action, retention, return engagement, community participation, usefulness, relevance, accessibility, conversion, referral, demand and post-course outcomes.

## RQ-015 Provenance
Maintain Atlas source → source record → generated content → asset → lesson/activity/assessment → credential traceability.

## RQ-016 Regeneration
When source/configuration changes, identify affected descendants and whether regeneration/review is required; preserve versions.

## RQ-017 Integrations
Represent integrations as capabilities/adapters. Verify actual connectivity and behaviour; do not mark an integration complete because configuration exists.

## RQ-018 Agent automation
Workers/agents should use idempotent, resumable, observable operations with receipts/evidence and minimal HITL.

## RQ-019 QA
Provide structural, unit, integration, end-to-end, accessibility, data-integrity and regression tests appropriate to the implemented surface.

## RQ-020 Governance/security
No secrets in source or generated reports. Private services remain private. Destructive/irreversible production operations require explicit authorization.

## RQ-021 Status integrity
Separate content presence, design, schema, build, generation, integration, testing, QA, production readiness and live state. Overall status is derived where practical.

## RQ-022 Complete-run discipline
Goose must inspect, preserve, implement, test, repair and continue. A plan is not completion. A representative demo is not proof that all 489 lessons have been generated.
